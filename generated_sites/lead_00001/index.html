Our Story

Port of Peri Peri brings the flame-grilled flavors of Portuguese-style peri peri to Bothell. Every bird is marinated for 24 hours in African bird's-eye chilies, herbs and lemon, then grilled over open flame — never fried, never frozen.

We're a locally owned family spot on Bothell Everett Hwy. Dine in, take out, or feed the whole team with a family platter. Come taste why 1,200+ neighbors gave us 4.6 stars.
